About Me

Hello!

I'm Amelia, a second-year university student majoring in English with minors in Culture and Technology Studies and Studio Art. My academic journey has been a rich tapestry of interests, exploring diverse fields such as digital humanities, women's writing, print history in art and literature, critical theory, comparative literature, and contemporary poetry. I find joy in the creative process, a passion that took root during my grade 11 art course when when I began to develop skills in realism by learning how to observe a subject matter and paint what I see. The excitement I felt inspired me to work harder and find ways to connect and express my ideas through art, often drawing inspiration from concepts I was learning and infusing them with personal meaning.

Through my university courses, my understanding of art has expanded and I've found myself increasingly drawn to contemporary art, digital art, and web design. My art portfolio is a reflection of my academic pursuits and the emotions that infuse my work. It offers a glimpse into my evolving identity as an artist and scholar, inviting viewers to join me as I explore how the digital realm becomes a canvas for expression when disciplines, languages, and images intertwine.

In the span of my short career, my artwork has been featured in the KW Art Gallery and won several awards, such as the Julie O'Rourke Memorial Scholarship.
